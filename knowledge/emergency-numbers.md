---
title: Emergency numbers in India (112 and 108)
term: 112, 108, ambulance, emergency
source: https://112.gov.in
---
112 is India's single emergency number for police, fire and medical emergencies. 108 is the emergency ambulance number
in most states. In an emergency, call one of these numbers first, before searching for a hospital.

Hospitals marked "emergency" in this app reported emergency services in their data source, but always call ahead if you
can, because information may be out of date.
