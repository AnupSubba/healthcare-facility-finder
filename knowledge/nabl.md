---
title: What is NABL accreditation for labs?
term: NABL
source: https://nabl-india.org
---
NABL is the National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories, also part of the Quality Council of
India. A diagnostic or pathology lab that is "NABL accredited" has shown that it follows recognised standards for
accurate and reliable testing.

Accreditation usually applies to specific tests, not every test a lab offers. You can check a lab's accreditation and
its list of accredited tests on the official NABL website.
