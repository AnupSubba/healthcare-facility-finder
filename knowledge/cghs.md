---
title: What is CGHS?
term: CGHS
source: https://cghs.mohfw.gov.in
---
CGHS is the Central Government Health Scheme. It provides health care to central government employees, pensioners
and their dependants through CGHS wellness centres and empanelled hospitals and labs in many cities.

If you are a CGHS beneficiary, check the official CGHS website for the current list of wellness centres and empanelled
facilities in your city.
