---
title: Clinic, hospital or health centre?
term: clinic, hospital, PHC, CHC, dispensary
source: https://www.mohfw.gov.in
---
A clinic or dispensary usually offers consultations and basic treatment without admitting patients overnight.
A hospital can admit patients and usually has more specialists and equipment. In rural areas, Primary Health Centres
(PHCs) and Community Health Centres (CHCs) are government facilities that provide basic and first-level care.

For a minor illness a clinic is often enough; for serious problems or emergencies, go to a hospital or call 112 / 108.
