---
title: Government vs private hospitals
term: government hospital, sarkari aspatal, private hospital
source: https://www.mohfw.gov.in
---
Government hospitals are run by the central government, a state government or a local body. Treatment there is free
or low cost for many services, though waiting times can be longer. Large government hospitals often have emergency
departments that are open day and night.

Private hospitals are run by companies, trusts or individuals and charge fees. Some private hospitals accept government
schemes such as PM-JAY or CGHS. In this app, "ownership unknown" means the data source did not say which type it is.
