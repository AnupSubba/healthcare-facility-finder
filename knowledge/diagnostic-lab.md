---
title: What does a diagnostic lab do?
term: lab, pathology lab, diagnostic centre
source: https://nabl-india.org
---
A diagnostic lab tests samples such as blood or urine, and some centres also do imaging like X-rays, ultrasound or
scans. A doctor's prescription is usually needed for many tests. Some tests need preparation, such as fasting, so ask
the lab or your doctor before you go. A NABL-accredited lab has been checked for testing quality.

This app helps you find labs; it cannot explain your test results. Please discuss results with your doctor.
