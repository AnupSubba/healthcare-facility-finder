---
title: How to use this app
term: help, how to search
source: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
---
Type what you need and where, in English or Hindi, for example "government hospital in Lucknow", "lab near 110017" or
"Pune mein clinic". You can add a pincode in the box above the search. Each result shows the address, phone number,
directions and where the information came from.

This app gives information only, not medical advice. It does not book appointments or show prices.
