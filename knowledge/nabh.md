---
title: What is NABH accreditation?
term: NABH
source: https://nabh.co
---
NABH stands for the National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers. It is part of the Quality
Council of India. A hospital or clinic that is "NABH accredited" has been checked by NABH against national standards
for patient safety and quality of care, such as infection control, safe use of medicines and patients' rights.

Accreditation is voluntary, so many good hospitals are not accredited. It is a useful sign of quality, but it does not
guarantee a particular treatment or result. You can confirm a hospital's current status on the official NABH website.
