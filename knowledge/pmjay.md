---
title: What is PM-JAY (Ayushman Bharat)?
term: PM-JAY, Ayushman Bharat, Ayushman card
source: https://pmjay.gov.in
---
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is a Government of India health assurance scheme. It covers
eligible families for hospital treatment at government and empanelled private hospitals, up to a yearly limit per
family, without paying at the hospital for covered treatments.

Eligibility depends on the scheme's rules. You can check whether you are eligible, and which hospitals are empanelled,
on the official PM-JAY website or by calling its helpline. This app does not yet show which hospitals are empanelled.
