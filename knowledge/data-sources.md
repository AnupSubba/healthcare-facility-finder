Listings come from open public data: OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License) and datasets
published on data.gov.in under the Government Open Data License - India. The data is refreshed every month.

Open data can be incomplete or out of date, so please call a facility before visiting. If you see a mistake, use the
"Report incorrect info" button on that listing.
