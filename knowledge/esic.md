---
title: What is ESIC?
term: ESIC, ESI hospital, ESI dispensary
source: https://www.esic.gov.in
---
ESIC is the Employees' State Insurance Corporation. Eligible employees and their families who are insured under ESI
can get treatment at ESI hospitals and dispensaries. Check the official ESIC website for eligibility and for the list
of ESI facilities in your area.
